How it works

  1. Paste your GitHub Pages link. The name fills in from your repository.
  2. Pick a place from the list. Places marked "Opening soon" aren't on sale yet.
  3. One payment, no renewals: £ for a standard name, £ for a trade name like removals.
  4. When the payment confirms on-chain, a DNS record points your name at your site. Then open your repository's Settings, then Pages, and enter the name as the custom domain.

Names that look like councils, police, health services, banks or government are never sold, and misuse leads to immediate takedown. Read the terms.
